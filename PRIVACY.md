# Veneer Pro Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Veneer Pro does not collect, store or share any personal information.

- **No accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.** The app contains no analytics or advertising code and does not track you.
- **Your files stay on your Mac.** Models, projects, textures and exports are read and written only where you choose. Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any server.
- **Online libraries.** When you browse the ambientCG or Poly Haven material and HDRI libraries, the app downloads files directly from those sites. Those requests go to the sites themselves and are covered by their own privacy policies. No information about you is sent beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address).
- **Sharing to iPhone or iPad.** "Share to iPhone or iPad" runs a temporary web server on your Mac, reachable only on your local network, so your own devices can open the model. It stops when you stop sharing, and nothing passes through the developer.
- **Settings** such as library folders and viewer preferences are stored only on your Mac.

## Contact
